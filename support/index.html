Quorum Support

Quorum is a civic records reader for iOS. It follows the councils, commissions, boards, and committees that decide what happens where you live and work, and tells you when the topics you care about come up.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or sign-in?

No. Quorum has no accounts and no sign-in. The app opens straight to the civic lane you pick during onboarding.

What data am I looking at?

This first release runs in demo mode. It ships with a small set of bundled sample jurisdictions so every screen — onboarding, the feed, an agenda tree, a timestamped transcript, saved topics, and search — can be explored without credentials. In demo mode the app makes no network request at all.

How do I connect live data?

Live data is optional and requires a Hamlet API key. Open Settings in the app, enter the key, and Quorum will use it to fetch from the Hamlet API. The key is stored only in the iOS Keychain and is sent only to the API it belongs to. You can remove it in Settings at any time to return to demo mode. Quorum does not provide a key.

How do alerts work?

Alerts are local notifications — there is no push server, no APNs, and no device registration. After onboarding, Quorum asks once for notification permission and then notifies you when a saved topic matches a followed body. You can also run a check on demand from Settings.

How do I turn alerts off?

Turn notifications off for Quorum in the iOS Settings app under Notifications. In Quorum, Settings shows whether alerts are allowed and links straight there. Either way, the rest of the app keeps working.

Where are my follows, topics, and saved data stored?

On your device. Your civic lane, the jurisdictions and bodies you follow, your saved topics, and any records synced for offline use stay in the app's own container. Deleting the app deletes them. Nothing is sent to the developer.

What does Quorum collect?

Nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

Which devices are supported?

Quorum is an iPhone app and requires iOS 26.0 or later.
